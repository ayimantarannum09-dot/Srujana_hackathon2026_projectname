# HisaabHoshiyaar

**Because Every Rupee Has a Tomorrow**

A cash-flow forecasting web app that helps small-business owners see cash shortages coming, test "what if" scenarios and compare fixes. Built for the Srujana 2026 Hackathon, Finance track (problem statement F-03).

## Overview
HisaabHoshiyaar helps small-business owners understand their future cash position. It shows the projected cash balance week by week, warns the owner before cash falls below a safe level, and lets them test problems and solutions before they happen.

It is built for owners of small businesses in India (retail, services, manufacturing and trading) who are profitable on paper but struggle when customers pay late and bills fall due at the wrong time.

Our friendly mascot, **Ollie the Owl**, spots upcoming shortages and alerts the owner.

The app has three screens:
1. **Cash Overview:** current balance, projected weekly balance and shortage alerts
2. **Money In & Out:** every expected and received payment in one place
3. **What-If & Fixes:** scenario testing and a side-by-side comparison of fixes

## Problem
A business can be profitable on paper and still miss payroll or loan instalments, because customer payments arrive late and expenses fall due at the wrong time. Small businesses rarely have tools to see these shortages in advance or to compare their options before the shortage happens.

We chose problem statement **F-03: Predictive Cash-Flow Management for Small Businesses**. Our solution shows current and projected cash, separates expected money from money actually received, accounts for payment delays, and lets the owner test scenarios and corrective actions.
